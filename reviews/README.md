# Independent reviews

Every Candidate must receive an independent reverse review before promotion. The reviewer reads the source directly and records one decision:

- `verify`: evidence is sufficient for promotion.
- `revise`: the candidate must be corrected or completed and reviewed again.
- `reject`: the claim should be retained under `rejected/` with a reason.

A review does not silently rewrite the Candidate and does not define the project's core-model list. Use `templates/review.md` and keep the review trail after later revisions.
